# Audience retention edit

Inputs are the previous MP4, storyboard, narration and factual sources. Audit actual decoded frames, timings and audio. Identify which question the first fifteen seconds creates and when the film answers it. Match each new fact to a shot, a graphic action or an intentional hold. Cut repeated background and leave space for a relevant human consequence.

Change the script and shot order in `storyboards/titanic-retention-edit.json` or the project's equivalent. Measure Kokoro narration before locking the cutting lengths. Render a matched preview, inspect shots at the new information points, and record specific differences from the baseline.

Deliver the revised cut and a timecoded audit. Validate historical claims, opening stakes, visual variety, caption fit and a clear chapter payoff. Reserve audience-retention claims for measured viewer analytics.
