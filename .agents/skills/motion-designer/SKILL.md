---
name: motion-designer
description: Design deterministic Remotion camera paths, depth-layer motion, title reveals and transitions for documentary scenes.
---

Map story beats to the supported camera presets and transition modes in the storyboard schema. Derive every movement from the local Sequence frame and fps. Use clamped interpolation for shots and a damped spring for typography. Keep handheld movement subordinate to the main camera path. Overlap incoming images at the start of their scene using visual support from the outgoing shot. Each narration and caption belongs only to its own scene.

Match cuts, reframing, and graphic reveals to changes in the narration or sound. Use a brief title reveal at the opening or a chapter boundary. Across adjacent shots, vary the scale, angle, or kind of visual evidence. Give important moments room to hold.
