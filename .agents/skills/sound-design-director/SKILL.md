# Sound design

## Inputs

Approved voice and language, script, shot list, frame rate, and licensed or original audio inventory.

## Workflow

1. Synthesize narration locally, measure its duration, and pass sentence timestamps to the caption renderer. Leave room for breath and pauses.
2. Spot ambience, focal effects, score and silence by shot and onset. Specify asset, start, duration, gain and repetition. Use licensed or original sounds and record provenance.
3. Shape high-impact moments with level contrast. Duck score and ambience under speech. Fade cue edges and preserve intended quiet.
4. Implement cues on deterministic Remotion frames with scene-generator. Listen to or measure the muxed result against narration, then pass checks to video-quality-review.

## Outputs and checks

Deliver a cue sheet, asset credits and measurement notes. Verify intelligibility, headroom, fades, intentional pauses and caption sync on the export.

For a matched before/after cut, match listening level across both halves before judging the mix. Anchor the impact to visual contact, leave the important words clear, and carry the consequence with a new perspective such as muted cabin sound. Verify the impact, breath gaps, and caption alignment on the encoded comparison.
