# Documentary story

## Inputs

Brief, audience, language, target duration, primary reporting, and any narration constraints.

## Workflow

1. Build a fact ledger with source, wording confidence, and disagreements. Mark inference and avoid invented dialogue or composite witnesses.
2. Write a short opening around a specific fact or decision and leave a question the film can answer. Give figures human consequence.
3. Outline the story as actions and consequences. Introduce background where it changes the viewer's understanding of an action.
4. Write speakable narration with changes of pace, room for silence, and a closing image or observation that answers the opening.
5. Estimate at the chosen voice's pace and revise against measured synthesis. Hand chapter boundaries, source notes, and narration to retention-editor and cinematic-shot-planner.

## Outputs and checks

Deliver a timed script, chapter goals and reporting references. Each chapter must change the audience's understanding. Read the script aloud, verify names and quantities, and flag every disputed or reconstructed claim.
