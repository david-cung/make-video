# Title and thumbnail

## Inputs

Script, strongest verified fact, intended audience, candidate footage or illustration, and distribution format.

## Workflow

1. Identify the film's promise in one sentence. Draft distinct candidate titles built around an event, decision, or verified question.
2. Pair each with one recognizable visual and at most a few words of necessary thumbnail text. Leave title-copy and image space deliberate.
3. Validate historical claims, subject identity and asset rights with visual-asset-researcher. If it is a reconstruction, make its treatment coherent with the film.
4. Inspect the artwork at phone size and test whether the opening fulfills the promise. Include a primary proposal and one alternate with rationale.
5. Pass the recommended title and thumbnail direction to video-quality-review. Export art only when requested by the brief.

## Outputs and checks

Deliver a title, thumbnail framing brief, source/rights references, and an alternate. Check small-size contrast, recognizable silhouette, and alignment with the first scene.
