# Visual evidence

## Inputs

Shot list, factual sources, asset inventory, distribution scope, and target dimensions.

## Workflow

1. Search archives and museums for the exact subject, date and view. Open the item record and inspect the actual image.
2. Record item URL, creator or stated unknown creator, date, license or rights advisory, credit and local path. Record restrictions and pursue another image when permission is unclear.
3. Check size, focus, crop and caption space. Specify authentic establishing views and legible details. Never pass a similar vessel, place or date off as the subject.
4. For missing views, design local original reconstructions using verified geometry or clearly schematic maps, cutaways and diagrams. Label the reconstruction where the viewer could mistake it for evidence.
5. Supply actual files and credits to visual-director and scene-generator. Give video-quality-review the same ledger.

## Outputs and checks

Deliver a shot-to-asset manifest and credits ledger. Audit identity, licensing, visual readability and origin on every used image.
