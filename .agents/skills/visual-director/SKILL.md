---
name: visual-director
description: Turn a short documentary script into a shot-by-shot storyboard with deliberate depth, lighting, palette, framing and sourced assets.
---

Read the storyboard schema and follow the image-first visual defaults in AGENTS.md. Give each shot a visual action that supports a specific beat in the narration. Establish the location at wide scale, then vary angle, distance, and subject placement to reveal information. Describe distinct foreground, midground, and background planes for parallax. Choose an unobtrusive label position for each framed subject and keep its important details clear of subtitles. List asset attribution and rights when using source imagery. Identify diagrams and reconstructions as illustrations.
