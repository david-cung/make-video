---
name: visual-director
description: Turn a short documentary script into a shot-by-shot storyboard with deliberate depth, lighting, palette, framing and sourced assets.
---

Read the storyboard schema before editing. Give each shot a visual action that supports the narration. Establish the location at wide scale, then vary the angle and distance to reveal information. Describe distinct foreground, midground and background planes for parallax. Reserve the lower caption area and place the main subject away from the title column. List asset attribution and rights when using source imagery. Identify diagrams and reconstructions as illustrations.
