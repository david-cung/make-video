# Visual and retention review

Extract and compare frames from the baseline MP4 and the resulting MP4 at matched story beats. Inspect the start, hold, cut and end of each shot. Review whether map and diagram actions explain narration, the subject stays clear of captions, and archive and reconstruction labels are legible.

Measure shot-length range, repeated patterns, time to first visual change and whether the announced mechanism resolves on screen. A frame difference is a motion signal. Give that signal context by inspecting frames with captions and labels outside the comparison region.

Verify encoded video/audio with FFprobe, scan for black/frozen intervals, and compare measured speech against the final audio. Report technical checks separately from editorial judgment and avoid inferring audience analytics from a short preview. The comparison runner records a media review and extracts representative frames for inspection.
