# Side Street Specials

A cosy, slightly crooked neighbourhood where a perfectly ordinary purchase can become a dispute about what an object technically is. The camera stays with the people long enough for us to see them decide how much dignity an argument is worth.

Jun likes Milo because a good argument makes the queue less boring. Milo keeps coming back because she is the one person who will explain why he is wrong down to the penny. Cross them with strong eye targets, and keep their left/right screen positions on the same side of the counter unless a wide shot establishes a new axis.

## World and art direction

The stall draws its palette from faded apricot tiles, bottle-green joinery, cream paper and warm rust. The broad graphic shapes stay legible in a close-up. Ink outlines separate limbs from clothing. No asset, branding or dialogue from the reference is part of the series. The sets include the noodle window, street, restaurant, apartment, living room, kitchen, convenience store, alley and courtyard. Each has day and night palettes, reusable placement anchors and a foreground plane. The noodle window is the pilot's fully dressed hero location.

## Stories and timing

Open on a choice or promise. Reveal the smallest exception, let the characters negotiate, then make the consequences visible. Leave a beat for the listener before cutting away. Finish on an object or expression that alters the meaning of the opening. For the pilot, the two-dollar bowl sign opens the episode. A long receipt becomes an edible alternative in Milo's mind, and the price returns as a new menu item.

## Acting and audio

Dialogue uses the actual generated WAV for timing. Eyelines, independent blinks, facial transitions and arm motion stay active underneath. Reach before a pickup, change ownership at contact and return the prop to the surface before releasing. Sound follows the action. The original score ducks under speech and drops out for a held reaction. The scripts describe line intention and adjust text, rate and pauses. The English adapter does not expose a dedicated anger, whisper or laugh control. Laughing and embarrassment can play as silent body and facial acting.
