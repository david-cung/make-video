# Titanic documentary sources and rights

The chronology uses approximate ship's times, and the narration rounds passenger and survivor totals where the historical records differ. The storyboard links each scene to its supporting sources. Reconstructions, schematic diagrams and archival photographs carry distinct labels in the film.

- [British Wreck Commissioner's Inquiry report, 1912](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRep01.php) and [United States Senate inquiry report, 1912](https://www.senate.gov/artandhistory/history/resources/pdf/TitanicReport.pdf) support the ship's specifications, collision, progressive flooding, boat evacuation, and rescue.
- [Royal Museums Greenwich research guide](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/research-guides/research-guide-d1-rms-titanic-fact-sheet) and [The National Archives' passenger records](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/teaching-resources/passengers-on-the-titanic/) support the voyage chronology, passengers, and rounded loss figures.
- [WHOI's 1985 expedition history](https://www.whoi.edu/ocean-learning-hub/ocean-topics/ocean-human-lives/underwater-archaeology/rms-titanic/1985-discovery-of-rms-titanic/) and [its 1986 follow-up](https://www.whoi.edu/ocean-learning-hub/ocean-topics/ocean-human-lives/underwater-archaeology/rms-titanic/1986-return-to-the-rms-titanic/) support the discovery and deep-sea exploration. [NOAA collates later expeditions](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/maritime/titanic/past_expeditions.html).
- [IMO's account of Titanic and SOLAS](https://wwwcdn.imo.org/localresources/en/OurWork/Safety/Documents/TITANIC.pdf) and the [U.S. Coast Guard's Ice Patrol report](https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/iip/2012_IIP_Annual_Report.pdf) document the maritime safety legacy.
