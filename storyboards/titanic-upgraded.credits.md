# Titanic: The Race for the Lifeboats

The storyboard carries source IDs for each shot. The 1912 British final report gives a total boat rating of 1,178. The script rounds the numbers aboard and rescued because the British and U.S. inquiries give slightly different totals. The sinking chronology uses approximate Titanic ship time. Arthur Rostron's first contact with a lifeboat uses Carpathia's clock. [British final report on the boats](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/BOTRepBoats.php) · [United States Senate report](https://www.senate.gov/about/resources/pdf/TitanicReport.pdf) · [Rostron testimony](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTInq28Rostron01.php)

The damage sequence follows the British finding that flooding in the first five forward compartments was enough to start progressive flooding. The cutaway compresses the relevant sections and uses original geometry. The route chart and approach are schematic. The wreck and collision scenes are illustrated reconstructions. [Wreck Commissioner's flooding analysis](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepObserv.php) · [Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution expedition history](https://www.whoi.edu/ocean-learning-hub/ocean-topics/ocean-human-lives/underwater-archaeology/rms-titanic/1985-discovery-of-rms-titanic/)

## Audio and reconstruction

The score and sound effects are original procedural compositions made locally with NumPy and FFmpeg. The ship profile, ocean animation, plans, maps and other reconstructions are original Remotion graphics. Kokoro's British English model performs the narration using `bm_george`. No third-party footage, music or sound recordings appear in the upgraded cut.
